Info

Move the paddle with your pointer or finger. Angle sets the bounce direction; spin curves the ball. The dotted line shows the opening serve direction, stopping before an obstacle.

A / D rotates and charges spin; X restores automatic angle. Space serves; P pauses; R restarts. Touch controls latch spin until No spin is selected.

Score past the opponent to remove health. Strong spin damages blocks more. Quick movement spends stamina and softens contact power; rest or clean-hit upgrades recover it. Choose Control, Power or Survival rewards to shape a run. Bosses alternate attack and receive phases.

Practice has unlimited health and does not save a score. Choose Start game when ready. Quantum effects are game rules inspired by physics, not a scientific simulation.
